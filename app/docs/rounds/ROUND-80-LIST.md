# Round 80 (Mac 4.12.0)

## Streaks and Today's standard, as one
- **A streak's rules join Today's standard** (as they did since Round 68). Now a rule can be **checked by the app**: work hours, a workout, two workouts, a calorie deficit, reaching out to a client or lead, the journal, or a weigh-in. Pick it per rule when you start a streak ("I tick it" or "App: …").
- **No duplicates:** "Work hours" is your deep work target, and "Workout" is the Trained chip. Neither is added again as a separate item.
- **Tracked items tick themselves** (on Today they say "auto", on the Streaks page "AUTO"). Tapping one doesn't override what the app sees.
- **Master streaks** (Personal → Streaks, above Today's standard), kept app-wide:
  - **Work hours:** your deep work target on work days. Days off don't count against it, and light or shoot days ask for half.
  - **Workout**
  - **Calorie deficit:** log what you eat and stay under the target.
  - **Reach out:** a client or lead, on work days.
  - **Journal**
- **On the master streaks:** each shows how many days in a row, your best run and the last 3 weeks. Turning one on puts it in Today's standard; "Stayed within calorie target" becomes the tracked calorie deficit.
- **New preset "Locked In 30":** 30 days of work hours, a workout, reaching out and the journal, all checked by the app.

## Health: the calorie goal
- **Calorie goal card** at the top of Health:
  - **Target:** what you burn a day (with an estimate from your weight) minus the deficit you pick (Easy −250 / Steady −500 / Hard −750) sets your daily target, and the rest of the app uses it.
  - **Progress:** eaten today, what's left, weeks to your goal weight.
- **Op's tip** depends on the moment (nothing logged by the afternoon, over the target, close to it at night), with "Ask Op for more". Op also knows the goal and gives specific tips.

## Your schedule (Personal → Schedule)
- **Turn it on and it runs your wake-up and work alarms**, day by day. A change you make for one morning or one day still wins. Turning it off puts the alarms back how they were.
- **Presets:** Locked in (six days on), Creator week (shoot Tue and Thu, edit Mon, Wed and Fri), Classic 5/2, Monk mode. Then change any day: Work, Shoot, Light or Day off, when you're up, and when work starts and ends.
- **Two weeks at a glance:** each day's kind, with holidays and vacations.
- **Should I say yes?** Say what it is (going out, a date, family, work or money, a favor), the day and the time, and it gives a straight yes or no with the reason, checked against your hours, tomorrow's start (when to leave by) and your limit on social nights a week. "I'm going" puts it on the calendar.
- **Your rules:** each preset comes with some (e.g. no plans before 6 PM on a work day). Edit them; Op holds you to them.
- **The month:** the money review on the 1st, plan the month on the first Monday, wrap the month on the last Friday (as tasks).
- **The year:** every holiday for the next 12 months as Off, Light or Work. Monday holidays default to work, a quiet day to get ahead, and the big ones are off. Vacations are date ranges with no alarms.
