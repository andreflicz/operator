# Round 66: Andre's updates (Nov 1)

## A. Playbooks
1. **A task added from a playbook is called "Next step".** It should carry the step's own name. Fix it everywhere a
   playbook task is made.
2. **"When they finish" is ugly** and should offer the playbook's other stages (or another playbook) as the options,
   not a bare dropdown.
3. **Remove "Start when a lead reaches".** Once a lead is in the system, they're on the lead playbook automatically.

## B. Calendar
4. **A Clients view**: every client's calendar at once (content, playbook steps, calls, tasks, billing), not one client
   at a time.

## C. Streaks
5. **Two separate things:**
   - **Day streaks** = working regularly, doing what you need to do every day (the fire streak and Today's standard).
     Holidays / time off: you can take days off or stretch your days off for a week without breaking it.
   - **Streaks (challenges)** = non-stop periods of doing something good for yourself, at different hardness and
     length. Look up more of them (beyond 75 Soft / Medium / Hard) and offer them by level.
6. **Starting a challenge is serious**: you lock into it (a commitment step, not one tap), and the section is
   inspirational and pushes you: quotes, what keeps grinding is worth (level / XP / "rank" difference if you keep
   going vs. if you stop), and so on.

## D. Today's widgets (screenshots)
7. **Widgets leave awkward empty space** (Business snapshot, Today's tasks and others look disfigured, don't fit or
   don't fill their space at S / M / L). Give every widget one shared set of styles so each one looks right at every
   size and fills its space.
8. **Your why**: it looks good but should be small (it's short text). Revamp the section, add to it if it helps, and
   make everything fit nicely.
9. **Leads to reach out to** (under Clients) still looks bad. Fix it.

## E. Journal vault
10. **Old vault journals weren't locked.** Lock the ones already in the vault, and make sure anything put in the vault is
    always locked from now on.

## F. Notifications
11. **A notifications system you can customize** (its own section in Settings):
    - what you get notified about, per kind: socials on your personal page (likes, comments, shares, follows), your
      clients getting leads, and everything the app already notifies about;
    - how each one shows up: a banner at the top of the screen (like the XP one), a note at the top right, or only in
      the list.
12. **Social notifications that look good**: likes, comments, shares etc. on your personal page, and when a client gets
    a new lead from their ad campaign.
13. **XP for your personal page's engagement** (likes, comments, shares), added to the XP system and the XP guide:
    small and steady, with a bigger bonus when a post takes off.
14. **Notifications live at the top right**: the bell moves into the player (top right), and notes come in from there.

## G. Social and ads
15. **The client report comes back, only here** (Social / ad campaigns): a custom-designed PDF report (what you see is
    the PDF you export), with room for your own words and notes from the client's journal.
16. **More from Zernio for ad campaigns**, whatever its data allows.
17. (Context: he mostly uses Business Manager to look at clients and post through their accounts, so this is where the
    report and the ad numbers belong.)

## H. Getting to know the user (added from Andre's answers)
18. **The app asks what it needs to know when it starts** (a setup for a new user, and once for Andre after this
    update): where you live, which holidays you celebrate, whether you take them off (and how many days), your usual
    days off. It uses the answers everywhere: day streaks skip your holidays and time off without breaking, the
    holiday banners and music follow your holidays, Op knows them. All of it can be changed in Settings later.

## Andre's answers
- **Time off for day streaks**: not a setting to hunt for; the app asks (item 18).
- **The report**: always a custom-made PDF, the one you see and the one you export, and it has to look incredible.
  You can add your own words: what you saw this month, and notes pulled from the client's journal.
- **XP for engagement**: small and steady, but a post that takes off (a spike in likes / comments / shares) earns a
  bigger bonus.
- **Client leads**: a new lead from their ad campaign.
