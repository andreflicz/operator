# Round 63: Streaks (Andre, Nov 1)

"Add somewhere in Personal called Streaks. That's where we change Today's standard (remove it everywhere else so it stays
in one place), and where we can start streaks: think 75 Hard, or a first streak, since I want to go on my first real
streak of doing things right in a while. Look up techniques."

## A. A Streaks tab in Personal
1. **Personal → Streaks**, next to Goals: one place for streaks.
2. **Today's standard lives here**: the deep work target, the standard's items (add, rename, remove) and the days off
   allowed a week. It's taken out of Settings → Goals & Standards. On Today, "Today's standard" opens Streaks.

## B. Starting a streak (a challenge)
3. **Pick one, or make your own**, each with its daily rules ready to edit before you start:
   - **First streak**: built for coming back. Three small daily wins (the two-minute version of each), 21 days, and
     "never miss twice" (one slip is fine; two in a row starts over). When you finish it, Streaks offers the next step up.
   - **75 Soft**, **75 Medium**, **75 Hard**: their usual rules (75 Hard: two 45-minute workouts, one outside; a diet,
     no alcohol or cheat meals; about a gallon of water; 10 pages of a non-fiction book; a progress photo. Miss
     anything and you're back to day 1).
   - **Your own**: a name, how many days, the daily rules, how strict.
4. **How strict**: "Back to day 1" (75 Hard), "Never miss twice", or "Just track it".
5. **Each rule can have its moment** ("After my coffee…", "At the gym, 7 AM"), the habit-stacking technique.
6. **Running**: day N of N, today's rules to check off, the run so far as a row of days, best run, restart, end. One
   streak at a time on top (more can run).
7. **On Today**: the running streak shows in the streak panel with today's rules to tick.
8. **Op** can check rules off ("I did my 10 pages") and start one when asked.

## C. Techniques (from looking it up), built in, not lectured
9. Never miss twice, the two-minute version, stacking onto something you already do, same time and place, a visible
   chain, and building up (First streak → 75 Soft → 75 Medium → 75 Hard). A short, tidy "How streaks stick" card on
   the tab, one line each.

## Judgment calls to confirm
- The 75 Hard / Soft / Medium rules are the commonly published versions (Andy Frisella's book is the source for 75 Hard;
  Soft and Medium have no official rules). They're editable before you start.
- Settings → "Goals & Standards" becomes "Goals & Targets" once the standard moves out.
