# Round 77 (Mac 4.9.0)

## Bed tags (the most important part)
- **Sleep tag:** if an alarm is ringing, it snoozes it. If a wake snooze is already pending, it says so and stays snoozed. Otherwise it marks you asleep ("Goodnight"). It never snoozes unless an alarm is actually up.
- **Awake tag:**
  - Alarm ringing (or snoozed): stops it and marks you up.
  - No alarm, still asleep or not up yet: marks you awake. The wake alarm is NOT cancelled and still goes off; tapping again while it rings stops it.
  - Already up and no alarm: the next tap starts Good morning.
- **Desk tag:** one toggle. Locked in → lock out; otherwise → lock in.
- **Door tag:** starts a break.
- Existing starter tags are migrated once to the new steps.

## Playbooks
- "Describe it. Op builds it." box at the top of New playbook. Op writes the stages, steps, due days, checklists, notes and monthly repeats.

## Phone
- New live hero: a lock-in ring with a ticking timer, the current task, day progress, tasks done, streak and quests. Lock and Break buttons sit side by side.
- The journal shows newest first, with date and time.

## Tasks look
- Changed only in Settings → Look & Sound → Appearance, with a preview card for each of the five looks. The switcher bar on Focus is gone.

## Clients
- Changing a client's cycle no longer touches the date you started working with them.
