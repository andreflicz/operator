# Round 75 (Mac 4.7.0)

**Command Center (replaces Minimal view)**
1. Command View is now the Command Center: one full screen, nothing to scroll (checked at 1440×900 and 1280×720).
   - Top: what you're doing, the clock, and one lock button.
   - Middle: Focus, today's tasks you can tick off, what's due, and today's calendar.
   - Bottom: Your time (full width), a rotating quote, and the journal bubble.
2. It opens by itself when you lock in and goes away when you lock out. Settings → Look & Sound → Command Center has the switch.
3. In the Mac app it covers the whole screen, with no title bar or menu bar, until you close it.
4. Minimal view is gone. M and the old buttons open the Command Center; the Minimal style picker in Settings is replaced.
5. No Start shooting, and no lock buttons except the one. Music is just a small "Pause" when something's playing.
6. Customize (✎): turn each panel on or off, the auto-open switch, the big timer, and "bring every quote back".
7. On a second screen, ticking tasks, Lock, the journal bubble and the next quote all work. Those presses run in the main app.

**Quotes**
8. Click any quote (Command Center, Today, morning) to change the words or who said it, or to remove it. "Facebook office poster" can go.
9. Quotes on the Command Center change every 40 seconds.

**Today**
10. Removed: the big clock block, the Quick journal panel, and the duplicates in the Command View panel (its own header, hours, note, standard, vision). You can turn the clock and Quick journal back on under Customize.
11. Today's progress bar ("2h 55m / 3h") sits with the streak, right above Tasks.
12. Your time is full width with bigger numbers, and no Start shooting.

**Tags**
13. One list and one "+ Add a tag": pick a ready-made tag or "Something else".
    - The separate hands-free section, "More to start from", and the NFC Tools instructions are gone.
    - Every tag shows its Shortcuts setup and has an optional "Where it lives".
    - Away from home, Op's voice and staying awake are folded into one "Tag settings" line.

**Money and the season**
14. Debt: a debt entered as negative now counts as owed. Credit cards from your bank that show a balance owed count toward All debt and are listed under Debts.
15. Season: no holiday box on top of Today (birthday keeps its line). The bunting hangs from its string and stops before the top-right buttons.
